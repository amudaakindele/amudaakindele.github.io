    About Me

    Advancing Trustworthy AI for Smart and Connected Health.

    I am a Healthcare AI Researcher, Ph.D. candidate in Information Technology, and Adjunct Faculty member in the Department of Computer & Information Sciences at Towson University. I am also an IEEE Senior Member. I am advised by Prof. Jinjuan Feng and collaborate closely with Prof. Lei Zhang (Towson University) and Prof. Qianlong Wang (Old Dominion University) on AI for Smart and Connected Health.

    My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, healthcare, and cybersecurity. I develop trustworthy and privacy-preserving machine learning methods for healthcare, including models designed to learn from data distributed across hospitals without sharing patient records. My work has appeared in peer-reviewed journals, including Algorithms, Springer book chapters, and international conference proceedings, including SERA, IEEE CAI, and ICCCN.

    Before joining Towson University, I earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Ibadan and a B.Sc.Ed. in Computer Science from Adekunle Ajasin University, Nigeria. I have taught computing courses in the United States and Nigeria and actively mentor emerging AI researchers through Black in AI, Code2040, and ML4H.

    Research Interests

    • Federated Learning: Privacy-preserving and personalized machine learning for heterogeneous (non-IID) clinical data.
    • Medical Imaging & Multimodal AI: Vision Transformers, graph neural networks, and hybrid deep learning architectures for medical image analysis and disease detection.
    • Trustworthy AI & Clinical NLP: Explainable and uncertainty-aware machine learning, medical document classification, and clinical text analysis.
    • Healthcare Cybersecurity: AI-driven threat detection and security for the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) and Internet of Medical Vehicles (IoMV).

    I welcome interdisciplinary research collaborations in trustworthy AI, healthcare, and intelligent connected systems. Feel free to reach out by email!
